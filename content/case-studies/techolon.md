Techolon is my personal YouTube channel, where I post video edits. Its tagline is "Just a guy that likes editing."

## Overview

Most of the channel is Rocket League montages: gameplay clips cut to music, with titles like *Fall*, *Animals*, *The River*, *Counting Stars*, *Someday* and *Lights*. One montage was made with clips from another player. The channel also has a three-part series of line animations (*The Explosion*, *Darkness*, *Rebirth*) and a holiday edit.
