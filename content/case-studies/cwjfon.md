I redesigned the Squarespace website for Central Washington Justice for Our Neighbors (CWJFON), a regional nonprofit, and site visits increased by 150%.

## The client

CWJFON is a Walla Walla–based nonprofit that provides accessible immigration legal services and does community organizing with immigrant communities in central and eastern Washington. It has been doing this work since 2018 and is an affiliate of the Immigration Law & Justice Network. The website has several audiences: people who need legal help, community members looking for clinics and trainings, supporters who want to donate, and job applicants.

## What I built

A redesign of the organisation's Squarespace site, built around those audiences.

## Results

Site visits went up by **150%** after the redesign.

## Tech

Squarespace.

## Status

The organisation has changed the site since I handed it off, and I no longer maintain it, so [cwjfon.org](https://cwjfon.org) today doesn't reflect my redesign.
