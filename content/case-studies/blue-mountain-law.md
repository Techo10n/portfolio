I designed and launched a mobile-first Framer website for Blue Mountain Law Office, a small law office in Walla Walla, Washington that advises residential landlords. Client inquiries rose by over 400% in the three months after launch.

## The client

Blue Mountain Law focused on one niche: legal guidance for residential landlords in the Walla Walla Valley and across Eastern Washington. Its audience is property owners trying to keep up with Washington's landlord/tenant laws, which have changed a lot in recent years. The site needed to explain what the office does, show why a landlord should hire an attorney instead of going it alone, and make it easy to ask for a consultation.

## What I built

A single-page site, built and hosted on Framer, with sections that move a visitor from "who is this?" to "contact them":

- **Hero and value cards.** A headline about trusted landlord-law help, then four cards covering the office's selling points: open communication, personalised service, local experience, and commitment to results.
- **About.** An attorney bio with a short preview and a "Read more…" expansion, so the page stays short on a phone.
- **Services.** Three blocks: comprehensive legal support, cost-effective help for property managers, and guidance through complex legal problems.
- **Recent law changes.** A plain-language explanation of recent legislative changes and why representing yourself can be risky.
- **Notice reference and FAQ.** A quick guide to the common residential tenant notices (pay or vacate, comply or vacate, notice to quit, rent increase, non-renewal) with their notice periods, followed by a FAQ answering common questions about serving notices.
- **Consultation request form.** Name, phone number, opposing party, whether there's an open court case, and a description, so the office gets the key facts before the first call.
- **Contact footer** with the office address.

## Design

I designed it mobile-first. Framer's breakpoints let me lay out each section separately for desktop, tablet and phone instead of just shrinking the desktop version. The dense legal content is broken into cards, short sections, and labelled groups in the notice reference so it's easy to scan.

## Results

Client inquiries grew by **over 400% in three months** after launch.
